QUESTIONS

Things people ask before they start.

Do I have to move all my customers to WhatsApp?

No. Glime works on both channels. When a customer is ready to buy, Glime invites them to continue on WhatsApp because payments, confirmations and delivery updates are easier there. If a customer prefers to stay on Instagram, the conversation can continue there.

Can I approve messages before they are sent?

Yes. In approval mode, Glime prepares each reply and waits for you. When you are comfortable, you can switch to automatic sending. You can change this at any time.

How do I know a payment is genuine?

An order is marked as paid only when your payment provider confirms the payment. A screenshot sent by a customer is never treated as proof.

What does Glime know about my business?

Only what you teach it. You add your services, prices, delivery details and common questions to one Knowledge Base, and every specialist uses it. Glime is designed to answer from that information.

Can I control how much Glime sends?

Yes. You can set daily limits for messages and AI usage on WhatsApp and Instagram, so usage always stays within what you are comfortable with.

What if a customer needs a human?

You can take over any conversation yourself at any time. Glime can also hand a conversation over to you when it needs your attention.

Is my Instagram or WhatsApp password shared?

No. Accounts are connected through each platform's official login, so your passwords are never shared with Glime. You can disconnect at any time.

Can I try it before paying?

Yes. Every new account starts with a 7-day free trial.
