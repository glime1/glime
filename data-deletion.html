GLIME DATA DELETION

Data Deletion Instructions

You can ask GLIME to delete the information associated with your account or with a connected WhatsApp Business or Instagram account. This page explains how to make the request, what is deleted, which records are kept for security, and what happens next.

01

How to request deletion

Option 1 – From your dashboard (business data). Log in, open Download Data in the sidebar and use Delete my business data. After you confirm your identity, the deletion is scheduled for 30 days later. You can cancel it at any time before that date. Your account and login stay active.

Delete from your dashboard Login required →

Option 2 – By email (full account or a connected channel). Send an email to our dedicated data-security address below, from the email address linked to your GLIME account. Use the subject line Data deletion request and tell us which account or connected channel (WhatsApp Business or Instagram) the request is about.

Request data deletion admin@glime.online →

We may ask for reasonable verification so that data is not deleted from the wrong account or by an unauthorized person. Before deleting, you can download a copy of your data from the Download Data page in your dashboard.

02

What gets deleted

Data we delete on request

  • WhatsApp chats: WhatsApp conversations and messages are deleted.
  • Instagram chats: Instagram conversations, messages and comments are deleted.
  • Voice conversations and messages, products and services, knowledge base items and enquiries.
  • Other business records: all leads, customers and orders that are not covered by the kept records described in section 03.
  • Email request: in addition, your GLIME account and profile information and business information saved in your account.
  • WhatsApp Business and Instagram connection details, including access tokens and identifiers, when you ask us to remove the connected channel.

You can also delete WhatsApp chats from the WhatsApp page and Instagram chats from the Instagram page of your dashboard at any time.

Data we may keep for a limited time

Billing, tax and accounting records, security and fraud-prevention logs, and the payment-proof records described in the next section may be kept where the law requires or permits it. This data is not used for any other purpose.

03

What is kept and why

When business data is deleted, we keep only the records of customers whose order is paid or processing, together with those orders and the leads linked to those customers. Everything else is deleted.

These records are kept for security and safety. If a customer later says they have paid and the business denies it, these records are needed to settle the matter fairly for both sides. They are used only for this purpose and for legal or accounting needs, are never used for marketing or sold, and are not shown to any other client.

Kept records are removed once they are no longer needed for these purposes, or earlier where the law requires. If you believe a record is being kept by mistake, write to admin@glime.online.

04

What happens next

01

Your request is recorded

Dashboard requests are confirmed on screen with the deletion date. For email requests we reply to confirm that we received the request and verify the account if needed.

02

We delete your data

Dashboard requests are deleted 30 days after you confirm them, unless you cancel before then. Email requests are deleted within 30 days of verifying your request. The records described in section 03 are kept.

03

We confirm completion

We email you once the deletion is complete. Deleted data cannot be recovered.

04

Long non-payment

If no successful payment is made to GLIME for 12 months after your last paid plan ended or your last successful payment, GLIME may permanently delete the data stored in that account. Where possible, we send a reminder to your registered email first. Download your data from the Download Data page before this happens. Deleted data cannot be recovered. The records described in section 03 may be kept.

05

Staying safe

  • Data requests are handled only from admin@glime.online. Be careful with emails that claim to be from GLIME but come from another address.
  • We never ask for your account password, login code or file password by email or chat.
  • Every download and deletion from the dashboard needs identity confirmation again, even if you are already logged in.
  • Downloaded files hold confidential business data. Lock them with a password and never share them on public links.
06

Remove GLIME from your Meta account

You can also stop GLIME from accessing your Facebook, Instagram or WhatsApp Business data at any time from your Meta settings:

  • Open Settings in your Facebook account and go to Business integrations (or Apps and websites).
  • Find GLIME in the list and choose Remove.
  • Then request deletion from your dashboard or email us at admin@glime.online if you also want the data already stored by GLIME to be deleted.
