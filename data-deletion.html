Data Deletion Instructions

You can ask GLIME to delete the information associated with your account or with a connected WhatsApp Business or Instagram account. This page explains how to make the request and what happens next.

Effective date 2 October 2026

01

How to request deletion

Send an email to the address below from the email address linked to your GLIME account where possible. Use the subject line Data deletion request and tell us which account or connected channel (WhatsApp Business or Instagram) the request is about.

Request data deletion connect@glime.online →

We may ask for reasonable verification so that data is not deleted from the wrong account or by an unauthorized person.

02

What gets deleted

Data we delete on request

  • Your GLIME account and profile information.
  • Business information saved in your GLIME account.
  • WhatsApp Business and Instagram connection details, including access tokens and identifiers.
  • Conversations, messages and related records stored by GLIME for your account.

Data we may keep for a limited time

Some records, such as billing, tax or accounting records, and security or fraud-prevention logs, may be kept for a limited period where the law requires or permits it. This data is not used for any other purpose.

03

What happens next

01

We confirm your request

We reply to your email to confirm that we received the request and verify the account if needed.

02

We delete your data

We delete the applicable data from our systems within 30 days of verifying your request.

03

We confirm completion

We email you once the deletion is complete.

04

Remove GLIME from your Meta account

You can also stop GLIME from accessing your Facebook, Instagram or WhatsApp Business data at any time from your Meta settings:

  • Open Settings in your Facebook account and go to Business integrations (or Apps and websites).
  • Find GLIME in the list and choose Remove.
  • Then email us at connect@glime.online if you also want the data already stored by GLIME to be deleted.
