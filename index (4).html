Security & compliance

Security that follows Indian law.

Customer conversations are personal data. GLIME is built to protect them and to follow the data protection rules that apply in India.

Protection built in

  • Data is held on secure cloud infrastructure
  • Secure authentication, access controls and data protection
  • No one browses your conversations. The GLIME team reviews only security alerts, and only the minimum needed, to keep the platform and every customer safe.

Misuse is stopped automatically

  • Automatic detection flags spam and misuse
  • Accounts that misuse the platform are blocked automatically
  • After a block, a review window opens so the owner can submit the case for review

Indian rules we build to

  • Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023, and the DPDP Rules, 2025, which roll out in phases
  • Information Technology Act, 2000, and the SPDI Rules, 2011
  • CERT-In cyber security directions, 2022
