FAQ

Common questions

Who can apply?

Marketing agencies, freelancers and consultants who manage WhatsApp or Instagram sales for businesses.

How do I pay GLIME?

You add money to a prepaid agency wallet. Each client plan charge is deducted from the wallet at your wholesale price.

Can I set my own price for clients?

Yes. You decide what to charge each client and collect it yourself. The dashboard records your price so it can show your margin.

What happens if my wallet runs out?

You get low-balance warnings first. If the wallet is still not topped up after the grace period, the affected client accounts are paused. They resume after you top up.

Does my client also earn me referral commission?

No. A client managed under an agency is not part of the referral program, so there is no double payment.

Can the client see my wholesale price?

No. Clients only see what you charge them.

Is white-label available?

Not yet. Your own logo, colours and domain are planned for a later release.
