What GLIME charges

One small fee for each sale, based on the course price. We never take a percentage of what you earn. The fee is taken from your GLIME wallet when a buyer pays.

If your wallet has less than the fee, buying is paused for that course and you get a message so you can add money. Buyers are never charged when a sale is paused.
