    What GLIME charges

    Add money

    One small fee for each sale, based on the course price. We never take a percentage of what you earn. The fee is taken from your GLIME wallet when a buyer pays.

    If your wallet has less than the fee, buying is paused for that course and you get a message so you can add money. Buyers are never charged when a sale is paused.

    How a buyer gets your course

    1Chat

    A buyer messages you on Instagram or WhatsApp. Your AI answers and shares the price.

    2Pay

    The AI sends a secure payment link. Money goes straight to your own account.

    3Link

    After payment the buyer automatically gets the private course link in the chat.

    4Code

    The buyer writes code and gets a 10-minute code. It opens the course on up to 2 devices.

    Let your AI sell it

    Add each published course to Services once. Choose delivery “GLIME secure course page”.

    Good to know

    • The course link only works with the buyer's mobile number and a fresh code, so a shared link alone cannot open it.
    • Every video, audio and PDF carries the buyer's number as a watermark, so a leak can be traced.
    • A course with paying students cannot be deleted. Remove lessons you no longer need instead.
    • Screen recording cannot be fully stopped by any website. The 2-device limit and the watermark discourage sharing.
