Relevant account, business and service data may be retained while it is needed to provide the requested features.

Applicable data is deleted or otherwise handled according to the request and the technical, contractual and legal requirements that apply.

Data deletion

You can request deletion of information associated with your GLIME account or an authorized WhatsApp Business connection by contacting:

Request data deletion connect@glime.online →

What you can request

  • Deletion of your GLIME account information.
  • Deletion of applicable business information.
  • Deletion of applicable WhatsApp connection information.
  • Deletion of applicable conversations, messages or related records, subject to legal or service requirements.
  • Deletion of other personal information that GLIME controls and is legally able to delete.

GLIME may request reasonable identity or account verification before completing a deletion request so that information is not deleted from the wrong account or by an unauthorized person.

Some information may remain for a limited period where retention is required or permitted by law, for accounting, security, fraud prevention, dispute resolution or other legitimate requirements.
