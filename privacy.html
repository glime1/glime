Privacy Policy

This Privacy Policy explains what information GLIME collects or processes when you use our AI business workspace, including the WhatsApp, Instagram, Shopify, payment and shipping integrations you choose to connect, how that information is used, shared, protected and deleted, and the choices you have.

01

Information GLIME collects

Depending on the features you use, GLIME collects or processes the information needed to create your account, connect your channels and tools, run conversations and orders, communicate with you, and keep the service secure and reliable.

01

Account information

Name, email address, sign-in details and other information linked to your GLIME account.

02

Business information

Business name and details, services, prices, FAQs, policies and other knowledge you add for your business.

03

Channel account information

Identifiers and connection details for your authorized WhatsApp Business and Instagram accounts, such as phone, account and page identifiers.

04

Conversations and messages

Message content, sender and recipient identifiers, usernames or phone numbers, timestamps, delivery or read status, message templates, and files or images that customers or you send in a conversation.

05

Customer, lead and order information

Details your customers share or you record, such as name, contact details, delivery address, interests, budget, products requested, leads, orders and follow-ups.

06

Store, payment and shipping information

Product catalog and order data from a connected store, payment and transaction status, and shipment details from connected shipping tools.

07

Files and media you upload

Images, videos and documents you add to services or send in conversations.

08

Voice information

Where you use voice features: call details and, where enabled, call transcripts or related records.

09

Usage, device & log information

Technical, security and diagnostic information such as device and browser type, pages used and error logs.

02

Connected platforms & tools

GLIME works with platforms that you choose to connect. We access only what the connection allows and what you authorize, and use it only for the features you turn on.

WA

WhatsApp Business Platform

Connect your account, show conversations, send and receive messages, and manage message templates.

IG

Instagram

Show your Instagram direct messages in GLIME, send replies, and understand which post or story a customer is asking about.

SH

Shopify

Read your product catalog so GLIME can show products and prices to customers in a conversation, and create orders in your store from those conversations and send the order details for processing.

RP

Razorpay and UPI payments

Process GLIME subscription payments and, where you connect your own account, collect payments from your customers. Card, UPI and bank details are entered with the payment provider; GLIME generally receives only payment status and transaction identifiers such as order and payment IDs.

SR

Shipping tools

Where you connect a shipping service, GLIME shares the delivery details needed to create and track shipments.

The platforms you connect have their own terms and privacy practices, which also apply to your use of them. Available integrations may change over time.

03

How GLIME uses information

GLIME uses information only as needed to provide, maintain, secure and improve the service and the features you have authorized and requested.

01

Provide the service

Create your account, maintain your connections, show conversations, send and receive messages, and manage services, leads, customers and orders.

02

Power AI replies and suggestions

Draft, suggest or send replies and sales follow-ups using your business information and the conversation.

03

Process orders and payments

Create and track orders, confirm payments, and coordinate shipping through the tools you connect.

04

Keep GLIME safe and reliable

Detect abuse and fraud, enforce our Acceptable Use Policy, fix problems and secure the platform.

05

Support, notices and legal duties

Respond to you, send service and security notices, handle billing, and meet legal, tax and accounting requirements.

04

AI processing

GLIME AI reads the conversation together with the services, prices and knowledge you have added for your business to write replies. To do this, relevant content is sent to AI service providers that process it on our behalf to generate the response.

AI
You stay in control

You choose whether the AI sends automatically, waits for your approval, or only suggests replies, and you can set daily limits. Your business information is kept under your account and is not used to answer customers of other businesses.

05

Your authorization & control

01 / USER CONTROL

You grant access

The business owner or an authorized administrator starts and approves every connection, such as WhatsApp, Instagram, Shopify or a payment account.

02 / LIMITED USE

Only the features you enable

GLIME uses connected-platform data only to provide the functionality you have turned on.

03 / DISCONNECT

You can stop at any time

You can disconnect a platform from Settings → Connector, or remove GLIME’s access from that platform’s own settings.

06

Storage, security & encryption

GLIME stores information on secure cloud infrastructure and applies technical and organizational controls intended to protect it from unauthorized access, loss, misuse or alteration. No internet or cloud system can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

Encryption Client data is encrypted in transit using HTTPS/TLS and is encrypted at rest on our cloud infrastructure.
Separate data for each business Access rules ensure a signed-in business can read and change only its own records.
Private file storage Uploaded images, videos and documents are kept in private storage and shown through temporary links.
Access controls Access to protected systems and data is limited by role and permission, and requires authentication.
Cloud infrastructure providers We use reputable cloud infrastructure providers for database, authentication, storage and server-side processing. Their platforms apply their own security controls.
Where data is stored Information may be stored and processed in cloud data centres in India or other countries, including Singapore.

See our Security page for more detail, including how to report a vulnerability.

SH

We may also disclose information when required by law or legal process, to protect rights and safety, or as part of a business transfer, with continued protection of your information.

08

Data retention

GLIME keeps information only as long as reasonably necessary for the purposes in this policy: to provide the service, maintain security, resolve disputes, comply with legal obligations, and meet legitimate accounting or business requirements.

01

While your account is active

Relevant account, business, conversation and order data is kept while it is needed to provide the features you use.

02

After you disconnect or ask for deletion

Applicable data is deleted or otherwise handled according to your request and the technical, contractual and legal requirements that apply.

03

Legal and accounting exceptions

Some information, such as billing and transaction records, may be kept for a limited period when required for legal, tax, accounting, fraud-prevention, security or dispute-resolution purposes.

10

Cookies & notifications

GLIME uses cookies and similar technologies that are needed to keep you signed in and the service working, and, where you allow it, to measure how the website is used. If you allow push notifications, we send alerts such as new messages to your device. You can change cookie choices and notification permissions at any time in your browser or device settings.
