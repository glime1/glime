01

Program overview

Creators are people who teach something — yoga, fitness, music, cooking, languages, business skills and more — and want to sell it without handling every chat, payment and delivery by hand. Creator Studio is the private workspace inside your GLIME client account where you build courses, see students and track earnings.

01

Creator Studio

Upload lessons, set a price, publish, and see your students, revenue and storage in one place.

02

AI that sells for you

GLIME AI answers buyer questions on Instagram and WhatsApp, shares the price and sends the secure payment link.

03

Your own store link

One link for your Instagram bio or YouTube description that shows all your published courses.

04

Protected delivery

Courses open only with the buyer’s mobile number and a fresh code, on up to 2 devices, with a traceable watermark.

You are the seller of your course. GLIME provides the software and infrastructure; the course, its price, its claims and its refund policy are yours.

02

How to join & verification

Any GLIME client can apply. Every application is checked by the GLIME team so buyers can trust that the person behind a course is real.

01

Open the Creator card

Sign in to your GLIME account, go to Settings → Connector and open the Creator card to start your application.

02

Add your public profile

Give at least one of your Instagram or YouTube links. You may give both. This is how the team confirms who you are.

03

Receive your passcode

The system generates a one-time passcode in the format GLIME-XXXXXX for your account.

04

Place it in your bio or description

Put the passcode in your Instagram bio or YouTube channel description and keep it there for 1–2 days so the GLIME team can see it on the profile you submitted.

05

GLIME team review

The team opens your profile, matches the passcode and checks that you are a real person with a real presence. The admin then approves or rejects the application.

06

Decision

If approved, Creator Studio opens in your account. If not approved, you receive the written reason and may correct the issue and apply again.

ID
Only real people, only their own profiles

You must apply with profiles that you own and control. Applying with someone else’s profile, a fake or borrowed identity, or a passcode placed by a third party is grounds for rejection, and for removal if it is found after approval.

03

What approved creators get

Approval is a decision by the GLIME team. Once you are approved, you receive:

01 / WELCOME GIFT

2 months of GLIME Growth plan, free

Every creator approved by the GLIME team receives two months of the GLIME Growth plan at no charge, as a one-time benefit for that creator.

02 / STUDIO

Full Creator Studio access

Courses, students, earnings, storage and your public store link, available from your own client account.

03 / AI SALES

AI selling in your chats

Add a published course to Services once and GLIME AI can offer it in every Instagram and WhatsApp conversation.

The free plan period is a promotional benefit. If approval was obtained with false information, GLIME may withdraw the benefit along with the creator status.

04

How selling works

From the first message to the first lesson, the buyer journey is automatic and protected.

1

Chat

A buyer messages you on Instagram or WhatsApp. GLIME AI answers using your course details and shares the price.

2

Pay

The AI sends a secure payment link. The buyer’s payment goes straight to your own account.

3

Link

After payment, the buyer automatically receives the private course link in the same chat.

4

Code

The buyer sends the word code on your WhatsApp and gets a 10-minute code that opens the course on up to 2 devices.

Only published courses appear in your store. Lesson limits: video up to 500 MB, audio up to 100 MB, PDF up to 50 MB per file, within your course storage.

06

Creator standards

Buyers trust the GLIME name when they pay a creator. These standards apply to every course, description, cover image, message and AI reply connected to your account, together with the Acceptable Use Policy.

01

Sell only what you own

Upload only content you created or have the written right to sell. Pirated, resold or copied courses are not allowed.

02

Describe it honestly

Course titles, descriptions and AI answers must match what the buyer actually receives. No fake results, fake testimonials or guaranteed income or health outcomes.

03

Deliver what you sell

Publish only complete courses you are ready to deliver. Keep lessons available and working for as long as buyers have access.

04

Stay within the law and platform rules

No illegal, adult, hateful, deceptive or dangerous content, and no content that Meta, YouTube or payment providers prohibit.

05

Respect buyers’ data

Use buyer names and numbers only to deliver the course, support the buyer and run your own business, never to spam, sell or share.

06

Be a real, reachable person

Keep your profile, contact details and payment account accurate, and answer buyer problems promptly.

For health, fitness, finance and similar subjects, courses are educational. Do not present them as licensed medical, legal or financial advice unless you are properly qualified and say so clearly.

07

Refunds & creator policies

Each creator is the seller of their own course, so each creator decides and publishes their own refund, cancellation and support policy for it. These policies are set by the creator through their own client account, not by GLIME.

What every creator must do

  • Set a clear refund and cancellation policy for your courses and keep it easy for buyers to find.
  • Tell buyers the policy before they pay, including through your AI’s answers.
  • Follow your own policy and the consumer-protection laws that apply to you.
  • Handle refunds and complaints promptly from your own payment account.
  • Never refuse a refund that your own policy or the law entitles the buyer to.

GLIME’s role

GLIME is not the seller and does not set course refund terms. The fee GLIME charges the creator is separate from the buyer’s purchase. If a creator ignores buyers or breaks their own promises, GLIME may act under the enforcement section below.

Buyers: contact the creator first, using the number or Instagram or YouTube profile you bought from. If a creator does not respond or you believe you were cheated, report it to GLIME using the contact details at the end of this page.

08

Buyer access & protection

Course content belongs to the creator. These controls protect both creators and buyers.

Number-bound access A course link alone cannot open the course. It needs the buyer’s mobile number and a fresh 10-minute code.
2-device limit Each purchase opens on up to 2 devices, which discourages account sharing.
Traceable watermark Videos, audio and PDFs carry the buyer’s number as a watermark so a leak can be traced.
01

Access period

Each buyer’s access runs for the period set for that purchase, shown in Creator Studio as an end date or lifetime access.

02

Courses with paying students

A course that has paying students cannot be deleted. A creator may remove individual lessons they no longer need.

03

No technology is perfect

No website can fully stop screen recording. GLIME’s limits and watermark discourage and trace misuse but cannot guarantee that content will never be copied.

04

Buyer obligations

Buyers may use a course only for their own learning. Sharing codes, accounts or content, or recording and reselling it, may lead to access being withdrawn.

09

Fraud & enforcement

Buyers and honest creators come first. If a creator cheats a buyer or abuses the program, GLIME may remove their access and may suspend or permanently remove their entire GLIME client account. GLIME may do this through automated safeguards, manual review by the GLIME team, or both.

ZERO
Zero tolerance for buyer fraud

Taking payment without delivering the course, selling fake or stolen content, using a false identity, or misleading buyers can lead to immediate action, without prior notice, to protect buyers, other creators and the platform.

Examples of conduct that can lead to action

  • Collecting payment and not delivering the course, or delivering something materially different from what was sold.
  • Selling pirated, copied or unauthorised content.
  • Fake identity, a profile that is not yours, or a passcode verification obtained dishonestly.
  • False or misleading claims, fake testimonials, or promises of guaranteed income or results.
  • Ignoring buyer complaints or refusing refunds the creator’s own policy or the law requires.
  • Repeated buyer complaints, payment disputes or chargebacks that point to unfair dealing.
  • Misusing buyer names, numbers or other data.
  • Trying to bypass fees, limits, access controls or verification.

Who is affected

Creator status is attached to your GLIME client account. When we act against a creator for fraud or serious misuse, the action can extend to the whole account and the channels connected to it.

Actions GLIME may take

01

Warn and ask for a fix

For minor or first-time issues, we may tell you what is wrong and give you time to correct it.

02

Pause or unpublish

Pause sales, unpublish a course, disable your store link or turn off AI selling while we investigate.

03

Revoke creator status

Remove Creator Studio access, and withdraw any free plan benefit that was given on the basis of the creator approval.

04

Suspend or remove the client account

For fraud, harm to buyers or repeated or serious violations, suspend or permanently remove the creator’s GLIME client account and disconnect connected channels.

05

Cooperate with authorities and providers

Share relevant information with payment providers, Meta, and law enforcement or other authorities where the law allows or requires, and support affected buyers.

GLIME may take these steps in any order that fits the risk, and immediately where needed for buyer safety, legal or platform reasons. Where possible we will tell you why. If you believe we made a mistake, write to connect@glime.online and we will review it. Removal of an account does not cancel money a creator owes to buyers, and GLIME is not responsible for that liability.

Privacy & data in the Creator Program

This section adds to the GLIME Privacy Policy, which also applies. It explains the extra information the Creator Program handles.

01

Creator application data

Your account and client ID, business name, Instagram and YouTube links, the generated passcode, review status and, if rejected, the reason. Used to verify you and run the program.

02

Course content

Lessons, descriptions, cover images and files you upload, and the storage they use. Used to host and deliver your courses.

03

Buyer data

Buyer name, mobile number, channel, course, amount paid, payment and access dates. Used to deliver the course, show the creator their students and prevent fraud.

04

Security data

Access codes, device checks and the watermark that carries the buyer’s number. Used to protect content and trace leaks.

01

What the creator sees

A creator can see the names and mobile numbers of their own paying students, and those numbers are also saved in the creator’s leads. The creator receives this data only to serve those buyers.

02

Creators must protect it

Creators must keep buyer data confidential, use it only for the course and lawful support, and follow applicable Indian data-protection law, including the Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023, where it applies.

03

We do not sell it

GLIME does not sell creator or buyer personal data. We share it only with service providers that help us run the program, with payment providers and Meta as needed for the service, and with authorities where the law requires or fraud investigation needs it.

04

Verification is public by design

Your passcode is placed on your own public profile for the team to check. Only place the passcode, never passwords, codes or private details, in your bio or description.

05

Retention and deletion

We keep program data as long as the account is active and as needed for payments, disputes, fraud prevention and legal duties. You can request deletion through the Data Deletion Instructions. Records needed to resolve fraud or legal claims may be kept for as long as the law and those purposes require.

06

Buyer rights

Buyers can ask the creator, or GLIME at connect@glime.online, to access, correct or delete their information, as the law allows.

Questions creators ask

Who can become a creator?

Any GLIME client who teaches a skill and has a real Instagram or YouTube presence can apply from Settings → Connector. The GLIME team approves each application after checking the passcode on your profile.

How long does the passcode need to stay in my bio?

Keep it in your Instagram bio or YouTube description for 1–2 days, or until the team confirms the check. After approval you can remove it.

Is the free Growth plan really free?

Yes. Approved creators get two months of the GLIME Growth plan at no charge. It is a one-time benefit for approved creators and may be withdrawn if approval was gained with false information.

Does GLIME take a commission on my sales?

No. GLIME charges only the flat fee per sale shown in the fees table, taken from your GLIME wallet. The buyer’s full payment goes to your own account.

Who decides the refund policy for my course?

You do. You set it for your own courses through your client account, share it with buyers before they pay, and honour it.

What happens if a creator cheats a buyer?

GLIME can pause sales, remove the creator status and suspend or remove the creator’s whole GLIME client account, and can cooperate with payment providers and authorities. See Fraud & enforcement above.

Can I delete a course that already has students?

No. A course with paying students cannot be deleted. You can remove individual lessons you no longer need.
