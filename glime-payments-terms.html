1. About these terms

These terms are between you (the business or person using GLIME) and GLIME. They apply together with GLIME's Terms of Service, Privacy Policy and Refund & Cancellation policy. If these terms conflict with the Terms of Service on the subject of payments, these terms apply.

You accept these terms by selecting "I agree" inside Settings > Payments. We record the version you accepted and the time. If you do not agree, do not use GLIME Payments. You can keep collecting payments with your own Razorpay account under Settings > Connector.

In short. Customers pay through GLIME's payment partner. A total fee is deducted from every payment and you receive the rest. You can withdraw only what you have actually received. A one-time KYC is required.

2. The service

  • GLIME Payments lets you collect payments from your customers inside your WhatsApp and Instagram conversations through GLIME, using Razorpay Route as the payment partner.
  • Razorpay creates a linked account for your business after your KYC. Each customer payment is transferred to that linked account after the fee in section 4 is deducted.
  • GLIME is a technology platform. GLIME is not a bank, does not accept deposits and does not hold your money as a wallet. Payment processing, identity verification and settlement are provided by Razorpay and its banking partners, whose own terms also apply to you.
  • GLIME may refuse, pause or change the service for any client where required by law, by Razorpay, or to protect customers and other clients.

3. Eligibility and KYC

  • You must be at least 18 years old, able to enter a binding contract, and legally allowed to carry on your business in India.
  • You must complete the one-time KYC in Settings > Payments with true and complete details: PAN, business details, address, contact details and a bank account in the name of your business or yourself.
  • For firms, LLPs and companies, we also need the PAN of an authorised person.
  • Razorpay verifies your details and decides whether to approve you. GLIME cannot guarantee approval. You can collect payments only after your status shows Verified.
  • You must keep your details up to date and tell us about any change. Changed bank or business details need fresh verification.
  • Giving false, borrowed or someone else's identity or bank details is a serious breach and may be reported to the authorities.

5. Collecting payments

  • Each payment is held for a hold period after it is received. This covers the refund and dispute window. The hold period for your account is shown in your dashboard.
  • Payments appear in Settings > Payments with their status: received, on hold, released, settled, refunded or failed.
  • You must send and deliver what the customer paid for. A payment is a payment for your goods or services, not an advance, loan or deposit.
  • You must not split, delay or route payments in a way meant to avoid fees, limits or checks.

6. Withdrawals and settlement

  • Only what you received. Your available balance is the net amount of your own customers' payments, minus refunds, minus amounts already withdrawn, and minus amounts still on hold. You can never withdraw more than that.
  • Money is paid only to the bank account verified during KYC. It cannot be sent to any other account.
  • Depending on how your account is set up, earnings are either settled to your bank automatically on Razorpay's schedule, or you request a withdrawal. Withdrawals have a minimum amount shown in your dashboard, and one withdrawal can be in progress at a time.
  • A withdrawal is processed through Razorpay and may take working days to reach your bank. Bank holidays and bank delays are outside GLIME's control.
  • If a withdrawal fails, the amount returns to your available balance.
  • GLIME may delay or freeze withdrawals while a payment, a refund, a dispute or a compliance review is open.

7. Refunds, disputes and chargebacks

  • You are responsible for refunds, disputes and chargebacks on payments you receive. A refunded or reversed amount is deducted from your balance, together with any charges Razorpay or the bank applies to it.
  • Whether the GLIME service fee on a refunded payment is returned is shown in your dashboard for that payment.
  • If your balance is not enough to cover a refund, dispute or chargeback, you agree that GLIME may deduct the shortfall from your future earnings and that you will pay any remaining amount within 7 days of our request.
  • Excessive refunds, disputes or chargebacks may lead to longer holds or suspension under section 12.

8. Permitted use

You may use GLIME Payments only for lawful goods and services that you are allowed to sell. You must not use it for:

  • anything illegal in India, counterfeit or stolen goods, or goods and services you do not have the right to sell;
  • businesses and products that Razorpay or the banking system prohibit or restrict, including gambling or betting, lending or deposit-taking without a licence, crypto assets, adult content, weapons, and similar restricted categories as set by Razorpay from time to time;
  • collecting money for another person or business, pooled funds, donations without a valid registration, or moving money for someone else;
  • fraud, money laundering, terrorist financing, test or fake transactions, or card testing;
  • misleading customers about what they are buying, the price or the delivery.

9. Your responsibilities

  • Give your customers accurate prices, product details and delivery information before they pay.
  • Deliver orders, handle customer complaints and issue invoices as the law requires.
  • Keep records of your orders and invoices. You may be asked to provide them to resolve a dispute.
  • Follow the Meta, WhatsApp and Instagram rules that apply to your messaging.
  • Cooperate with reasonable KYC, risk and compliance requests from GLIME and Razorpay.

10. Account security

  • Keep your GLIME login private. Anyone who can log in to your account can see your payments and request withdrawals.
  • Never share your password, one-time codes or session with anyone, including people who say they are from GLIME. GLIME will never ask for them.
  • Tell us immediately at if you suspect unauthorised access. We may freeze withdrawals while we investigate.
  • You are responsible for activity done through your account unless you told us about the unauthorised access first and we had a reasonable time to act.

  • More details are in the Privacy Policy.
