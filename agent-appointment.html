FAQ

Questions about the Appointment Agent.

What does the Appointment Agent do?

It books appointments from your free slots in chat, confirms them on WhatsApp and sends reminders 24 hours and 1 hour before.

Where do I see the bookings?

In your Calendar. It shows today's bookings, how many the AI booked, what is awaiting approval and your free slots.

Can I have more than one person or resource?

Yes. You can see who is free and filter bookings by each person or resource.

What reminders are sent?

A WhatsApp confirmation when a booking is confirmed, a reminder 24 hours before, a reminder 1 hour before and a thank-you after you mark the visit completed. Each one has its own on/off switch.

Why do I need a WhatsApp template?

WhatsApp lets you message someone who has not written in the last 24 hours only with a template approved by Meta. You send the text for approval once, and reminders use it automatically after that.

Which languages does it reply in?

Any language your customer writes in.

Are chat replies charged per message?

No. Messages inside the 24-hour window are free. You only buy AI credits for the AI you use.

Is it included in my plan?

It is one of the six specialists listed with GLIME Growth and Omni. See the Packages page for details.
