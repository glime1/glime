FAQ

Questions partners ask first.

Does it cost anything to join?

No. The Partner Program is free to join, and there are no minimum sales targets.

Do I need a GLIME client account?

No. Partner accounts are separate from client accounts. You sign in with Google and go straight to the partner dashboard.

How is commission calculated?

You earn a percentage of the subscription fee GLIME actually receives from each customer you refer, for life: every month the customer stays on a paid plan, starting from their first paid payment. The percentage depends on your tier.

When do I get paid?

Commission becomes available after a 30-day hold period. Available earnings are paid monthly on the 10th once your balance reaches the minimum payout of ₹1,000.

Can I refer myself?

No. Self-referrals and accounts you control are not eligible. See the program policies in the Partner Guide.

Where can I read the full rules?

In the Partner Guide, including the Partner Terms.
