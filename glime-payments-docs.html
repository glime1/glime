GLIME Payments Guide

GLIME Payments lets your customers pay you inside WhatsApp and Instagram chats without you needing your own Razorpay account. This guide explains how the money moves, what it costs, and how to stay safe. Version , .

Overview

Normally a business connects its own Razorpay account to GLIME. With GLIME Payments you do not need one. You complete a one-time KYC, GLIME and Razorpay create a payment account for your business, and customers pay through GLIME's payment partner. You see every payment, fee and balance in Settings > Payments and you only ever receive money that your own customers paid.

How it works

1. Customer paysIn the chat, your customer gets an order summary and a payment link and pays by UPI, card or netbanking.
2. Razorpay collectsThe payment is captured by GLIME's payment partner and recorded against your account.
3. Fee is deductedRazorpay's charges and GLIME's service fee are deducted. The rest is your net amount.
4. Hold periodYour net amount is held briefly to cover the refund and dispute window. It then becomes available.
5. Money reaches your bankDepending on your account, it is settled automatically or you withdraw it. It goes only to your verified bank account.

GLIME is a technology platform, not a bank. Payments are processed and settled by Razorpay and its banking partners.

Getting started

  1. Open Payments settings

    Go to Settings > Payments. If you do not see it or it says GLIME Payments is not enabled, contact us. It is switched on per account.

  2. Choose GLIME Payments

    Select Use GLIME Payments. The Terms & Conditions open first with your exact fee. Read to the end, tick both boxes and continue.

  3. Complete the KYC

    Fill in three short steps: business, contact and bank. See KYC below.

  4. Wait for verification

    Razorpay verifies your details, usually in 1 to 3 working days. Status shows Under review, then Verified.

  5. Start collecting

    Once verified, payment links created in your chats use GLIME Payments. Track everything in the Overview, Payments and Payouts tabs.

KYC

KYC is the identity and bank check required before anyone can receive customer money. You do it once.

What you need

  • Business type: individual, proprietorship, partnership firm, LLP or private limited company.
  • PAN, and the legal name exactly as on the PAN. Firms and companies also need an authorised person's PAN.
  • GSTIN, only if you have one.
  • Contact name, email, a mobile number and your full address with pincode.
  • A bank account number and IFSC. The account holder name must match your PAN name or business name.

Withdrawals

  • Two modes. Some accounts are settled automatically on Razorpay's schedule and need no action. Others have a Withdraw box in the Overview tab. Which one you have is shown in your dashboard.
  • Amount. Enter an amount up to your available balance, or tap Max. There is a minimum withdrawal, shown in the dashboard.
  • One at a time. You can have one withdrawal in progress. Request another after it finishes.
  • Destination. Only your verified bank account. To change it, contact support. A new bank needs fresh verification.
  • Timing. Withdrawals show Pending, Processing and then Paid with a bank reference (UTR). Banks can take working days.
  • Failed withdrawals. The amount returns to your available balance.
  • Safety. You are asked to confirm every withdrawal, and double submissions are blocked automatically.

Refunds and chargebacks

  • If a customer is refunded or a payment is reversed, the amount is deducted from your balance with any charges the bank or Razorpay applies.
  • If your balance is not enough, the shortfall is taken from your future earnings. See section 7 of the Terms.
  • The hold period exists so that most refunds are settled before money is withdrawn.
  • Keep your orders and invoices. If a customer disputes a payment, they help resolve it.

Security

What GLIME does

  • Payments are processed by Razorpay. GLIME does not see or store card numbers.
  • Balances and withdrawal limits are enforced on the server, so a withdrawal can never be more than your available balance.
  • Each withdrawal request is protected against double submission.
  • Every payment, fee, refund and withdrawal is written to an append-only ledger and checked against Razorpay.
  • Your PAN and bank number are never stored in full by GLIME. Your session must be signed in to see any of this.
  • Payouts can be frozen immediately if fraud is suspected.

What you should do

  • Never share your password or one-time codes. GLIME never asks for them.
  • Log out on shared devices and use a screen lock on your phone.
  • Check that the address bar shows glime.online before entering any details.
  • Write to right away if you see a payment or withdrawal you do not recognise.

Frequently asked questions

Do I need my own Razorpay account?

No. That is the point of GLIME Payments. If you already have one and prefer it, keep using it under Connector.

Is the 0.5% the only fee?

No. is GLIME's own service fee. Razorpay's charges are deducted too. The total for your account is shown in Settings > Payments before you accept the terms.

Can I withdraw more than I earned?

Never. You can withdraw only your available balance, which is calculated by the server from your customers' payments.

Why is some money "on hold"?

Each payment is held briefly to cover refunds and disputes. It becomes available automatically afterwards.

Can the money go to someone else's bank account?

No. Only to the bank account verified in your KYC.

How long does KYC take?

Usually 1 to 3 working days after you submit correct details.

KYC says "More information needed". What now?

Read the reason shown, correct the details and submit again.

What if a customer asks for a refund?

Refund through your normal process. The amount is deducted from your balance as explained in the terms.

I changed my bank account. What should I do?

Contact support. A new bank account needs fresh verification before payouts go there.

I do not recognise a withdrawal.

Write to immediately and change your password. We can freeze payouts while we check.

Which businesses are not allowed?

Anything illegal or restricted by Razorpay and the banking system. See section 8 of the Terms.
