Overview

Usually a business has to open and connect its own payment gateway account before it can take payments online. With GLIME Payments you skip that. You complete a one-time KYC, and customers pay you through GLIME. Every payment, fee and balance is visible in Settings > GLIME Payments, and you only ever receive money that your own customers paid.

How it works

1. Customer paysIn the chat, your customer gets an order summary and a payment link and pays by UPI, card or netbanking.
2. GLIME collectsThe payment is collected by GLIME and recorded against your account straight away.
3. Fee is deductedPayment processing charges and GLIME's service fee are deducted. The rest is your net amount.
4. Hold periodYour net amount is held briefly to cover the refund and dispute window. It then becomes available.
5. Payout to your bankYour available balance is paid out to your verified bank account, automatically or when you request it.

GLIME is a technology platform, not a bank. Payments are processed and paid out with the help of GLIME's regulated payment and banking partners.

Getting started

  1. Open GLIME Payments

    Go to Settings > GLIME Payments. If it says GLIME Payments is not enabled for your account, contact us. It is switched on per account.

  2. Choose GLIME Payments

    Select Use GLIME Payments. The key terms open first, with your exact fee. Read to the end, tick both boxes and continue.

  3. Complete the KYC

    Fill in four short steps: business, contact, bank and a final review. See KYC below.

  4. Wait for verification

    Your details are verified by our partner, usually in 1 to 3 working days. The status shows Under review, then Verified. The page updates by itself.

  5. Start collecting

    Once verified, payment links created in your chats use GLIME Payments. Follow everything in the Overview, Payments and Payouts tabs.

KYC

KYC is the identity and bank check required before anyone can receive customer money. You do it once.

What you need

  • Business type: individual, proprietorship, partnership firm, LLP or private limited company.
  • PAN, and the legal name exactly as on the PAN. Firms and companies also need an authorised person's PAN.
  • GSTIN, only if you have one.
  • Contact name, email, a mobile number and your full address with pincode.
  • A bank account number and IFSC. The account holder name must match your PAN name or business name.

Payouts

  • Two ways. Some accounts are paid out to the bank automatically and need no action. Others have a Withdraw box in the Overview tab. Your dashboard shows which one you have.
  • Amount. Enter an amount up to your available balance, or tap Max. There is a minimum amount, shown in the dashboard.
  • One at a time. One payout can be in progress. Request another after it finishes.
  • Destination. Only your verified bank account. To change it, contact support. A new bank account needs fresh verification.
  • Timing. A payout shows Pending, then Processing, then Paid with a bank reference (UTR). Banks can take working days.
  • Failed payouts. The amount returns to your available balance.
  • Safety. You are asked to confirm every payout request, and accidental double submissions are blocked automatically.

Refunds and chargebacks

  • If a customer is refunded or a payment is reversed, the amount is deducted from your balance with any charges the bank or payment partners apply.
  • If your balance is not enough, the shortfall is taken from your future earnings. See section 7 of the Terms.
  • The hold period exists so that most refunds are settled before money is paid out.
  • Keep your orders and invoices. If a customer disputes a payment, they help resolve it.

Security

What GLIME does

  • Customers pay on a secure payment page. GLIME does not see or store card numbers.
  • Balances and payout limits are enforced on the server, so a payout can never be more than your available balance.
  • Each payout request is protected against double submission.
  • Every payment, fee, refund and payout is written to an append-only ledger and checked regularly against our payment partners' records.
  • Your PAN and bank number are never stored in full by GLIME. You must be signed in to see any of this.
  • Payouts can be frozen immediately if fraud is suspected.

What you should do

  • Never share your password or one-time codes. GLIME never asks for them.
  • Log out on shared devices and use a screen lock on your phone.
  • Check that the address bar shows glime.online before entering any details.
  • Write to right away if you see a payment or payout you do not recognise.

Frequently asked questions

Do I need my own payment gateway account?

No. That is the point of GLIME Payments. If you already have a gateway account and prefer it, you can keep using it under Connector.

Is GLIME's service fee the only fee?

No. Along with GLIME's service fee of , payment processing charges of about (GST included) are deducted. The total for your account is shown in Settings before you accept the terms.

Can I take out more than I earned?

Never. You can pay out only your available balance, which the server calculates from your customers' payments.

Why is some money "on hold"?

Each payment is held briefly to cover refunds and disputes. It becomes available automatically afterwards.

Can the money go to someone else's bank account?

No. Only to the bank account verified in your KYC.

How long does KYC take?

Usually 1 to 3 working days after you submit correct details.

KYC says "More information needed". What now?

Read the reason shown, correct the details and submit again.

What if a customer asks for a refund?

Refund through your normal process. The amount is deducted from your balance, as explained in the terms.

I changed my bank account. What should I do?

Contact support. A new bank account needs fresh verification before payouts go there.

I do not recognise a payout.

Write to immediately and change your password. We can freeze payouts while we check.

Which businesses are not allowed?

Anything illegal or restricted by our payment partners, card networks and banks. See section 8 of the Terms.
