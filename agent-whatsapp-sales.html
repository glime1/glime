FAQ

Questions about the WhatsApp agent.

Which agent can take payments?

Only the WhatsApp Sales Specialist. It creates the order, sends a secure Razorpay payment link in the chat and sends the invoice after payment. The other agents never collect money.

Where does the money go?

Customers pay through Razorpay and the money goes to your own Razorpay account, not to GLIME. You connect your account once during setup.

Whose logo is on the invoice?

Yours. The invoice is built automatically from your own business name, logo and details together with the order your customer placed. It is not a GLIME invoice.

Does the customer get delivery updates?

Yes. When your courier status changes, the agent sends the update in the same WhatsApp chat, so customers do not need to ask where their order is.

What if the agent does not know an answer?

It does not guess. It answers only from the business details you add and, when it is not sure, it hands the chat to you so you can reply yourself.

Can I stop the agent at any time?

Yes. You can take over any chat, set limits in Settings and use the emergency stop to pause automatic replies.

Is there a time limit for replying on WhatsApp?

WhatsApp has a 24-hour customer-service window. On the Growth plan the agent replies inside this window. On the Omni plan it can message customers at any time.
