01 · OVERVIEW

What Glime does

Glime is an AI business automation platform for businesses that sell through Instagram and WhatsApp. Customers comment or message you, and Glime replies, sells, collects payment, creates the order and invoice, keeps the customer updated on delivery, and follows up later so they buy again.

Instead of one general chatbot, Glime is a team of specialists that share one brain and one customer record:

In one line. Glime takes a customer from first comment to delivered order and repeat purchase, while you stay in control.

02 · GETTING STARTED

From sign up to your first automated sale

  1. Create your account. Sign up on the free trial page. Every new account starts with a 7-day free trial.
  2. Connect your accounts. Link your Instagram and WhatsApp Business accounts using their official login.
  3. Add your business knowledge. Enter your services or products, prices, delivery details and common customer questions.
  4. Choose your sending mode. Approve every reply yourself, or let Glime send automatically.
  5. Connect payments. Link your payment account so Glime can send payment links and confirm payments.
  6. Go live. New comments and messages are picked up by Glime from this moment.
Tip. Start in approval mode for the first few days. Watch what Glime proposes, improve your Knowledge Base where needed, then switch to automatic sending when you are happy.

03 · CONNECTING ACCOUNTS

Connecting Instagram and WhatsApp

Glime connects to your accounts through the official login of each platform. This means you sign in on the platform's own screen and approve access. Glime never asks for, sees or stores your passwords.

Instagram

  • Use a business or professional Instagram account.
  • Sign in through the official Instagram login when asked and approve the permissions requested.
  • Once connected, Glime can read and reply to comments and direct messages on that account.

WhatsApp Business

  • Use a WhatsApp Business number you control.
  • Complete the official connection steps. The Integration Guide in your dashboard walks you through them.
  • After connecting, customer chats on that number appear in your WhatsApp Sales Specialist.

Disconnecting

You can disconnect any account at any time. When you disconnect, Glime stops reading and replying on that account.

Important. Keep your connected accounts in good standing with the platform. Glime follows each platform's rules, and the platform's own policies still apply to your accounts.

04 · KNOWLEDGE BASE AND AI BRAIN

One brain for every channel

Glime keeps one central AI Brain and one central Knowledge Base for your business. Instagram, WhatsApp and the follow-up specialist all read from it. That is why your customers get the same answers and the same tone everywhere.

What to put in the Knowledge Base

  • Products or services with clear names, descriptions and prices.
  • Delivery and returns details such as areas served, delivery time and return rules.
  • Customer support answers for the questions your customers ask most, added one topic at a time.
  • Business details such as timings, location and how to contact you.

How it is used

When a customer asks something, Glime looks at what you have taught it and answers from that. The more complete your Knowledge Base, the better and more accurate the replies.

Where to manage it

Open Settings and go to the AI section. It contains AI Behaviour, Knowledge Base, Channels and AI Access, Human Handoff and AI Health.

Good habit. Whenever a customer asks something Glime could not answer well, add that answer to the Knowledge Base once. Every channel learns it.

05 · INSTAGRAM SALES SPECIALIST

From comment to conversation

The Instagram Sales Specialist handles everything that happens on Instagram.

Comments

When someone comments on your post or reel asking about price or availability, Glime can reply on the comment and open a private conversation with that person in their inbox, so the sale continues in private.

Direct messages

In DMs, Glime answers instantly using your Knowledge Base, asks the right questions to understand what the customer wants, and handles doubts.

Lead capture and attribution

  • Every interested person is saved as a lead with their conversation history.
  • Glime keeps track of the content that brought the customer, so you can see which posts bring sales.

Modes

In approval mode, Glime prepares each reply and waits for you. In automatic mode, Glime sends replies on its own within the limits you set.

06 · WHATSAPP SALES SPECIALIST

Closing the sale on WhatsApp

The WhatsApp Sales Specialist manages customer chats on your WhatsApp Business number.

  • Customer chats with full conversation history and customer context.
  • Suggested or automatic replies depending on the mode you choose.
  • Order details such as item, quantity and delivery address are confirmed in chat.
  • Payment links are sent when the customer is ready to pay.
  • Confirmations and updates are sent after payment and during delivery.
About the 24-hour window. WhatsApp has its own rules about when free-form business messages can be sent after a customer's last message. Which messages Glime can send at what time depends on those rules and on your package. Check your package details for specifics.

07 · HANDOVER

From Instagram to WhatsApp, without starting over

When an Instagram customer is ready to buy, Glime invites them to continue on WhatsApp, where payment links, confirmations, invoices and delivery updates are easier to share and find later.

  1. The customer shows interest on Instagram and chats with the Instagram specialist.
  2. Glime saves what it learned: item, size, city and any other details.
  3. Glime invites the customer to continue on WhatsApp.
  4. The WhatsApp specialist opens the chat already knowing the context, so the customer does not repeat anything.
  5. Instagram and WhatsApp conversations are linked to the same lead.

08 · PAYMENTS

Collecting money safely

Glime works with Razorpay and UPI for collecting payments. The customer receives a payment link in the chat and pays in a few taps.

How payment confirmation works

An order is marked as paid only when your payment provider confirms the payment. Glime never treats a screenshot, a forwarded message or a customer's claim as proof of payment.

  1. Glime prepares the payment for the exact order amount.
  2. The customer receives the link and pays.
  3. The payment provider confirms the payment to Glime.
  4. Glime marks the order as paid and sends the confirmation message.

Money goes to your own connected payment account. If a payment stays pending, Glime can send a gentle reminder.

Never rely on screenshots. If a customer says they paid but the order is still unpaid, check your payment provider's dashboard before sending the product.

09 · ORDERS, INVOICES AND DELIVERY

From confirmed payment to doorstep

Order creation

After payment is confirmed, the order is recorded with the customer, items, delivery address and payment status. Orders can also come from the website or be added manually, and all of them appear together in the Orders page.

Confirmation message

The customer gets an order confirmation message on WhatsApp as soon as payment is confirmed.

Invoice

An invoice for the order is prepared and shared with the customer, so you do not have to create it by hand and your records stay clean.

Shipment and delivery

If you connect a shipping partner such as Shiprocket Cargo, shipment details are shared with the customer so they know when to expect delivery. The order status is tracked in your Orders page.

10 · FOLLOW-UPS AND REPEAT SALES

The sale is not the end

The Follow-up Specialist makes sure no lead and no customer is forgotten.

After a purchase

  • A few days later: a friendly check-in about the order.
  • After about one month: a message about new products or offers.
  • After about two months: a reminder to reorder.

Timing and wording are examples of the idea. You control what is sent and when.

For leads who did not buy

People who showed interest but went quiet receive follow-ups as well, so warm leads are not lost.

Controls

  • Manual approval: you review every follow-up draft before it is sent.
  • Automatic sending: follow-ups go out after a valid trigger.
  • Per-lead switch: turn follow-ups on or off for a single lead.
  • Templates and analytics: reuse messages and see what works.

11 · LEADS

Know who is ready to buy

The Leads page shows every lead in a pipeline with the stages New, Contacted, Qualified, Follow-up Due, Won and Lost. Each card shows the source, priority, last activity and next follow-up.

  • Side panel: click a lead to see contact details, enquiry, conversation, notes and tasks.
  • AI insights: Glime explains why a lead looks promising and suggests the next action.
  • Source analytics: compare Instagram, WhatsApp, website and other sources.

12 · CLIENT ASSISTANT

Your business partner in the dashboard

The Client Assistant is a conversational helper that understands your business data.

What you can ask

  • Explain my data. "Where am I losing the most customers?" or "How were my sales this month?"
  • Analyse competitors. Ask how others in your space sell and position themselves, and get takeaways you can act on.
  • Prepare messages. "Send a reminder to customers who asked for a price last week."

Approve and send

When you ask the assistant to message customers, it shows you a proposal with the message and who will receive it. Nothing is sent until you press approve. After approval, the assistant sends it for you.

You decide. The assistant proposes and prepares. You approve. That keeps speed and control together.

17 · TROUBLESHOOTING AND FAQ

Common questions

Glime is not replying to a customer

Check that the account is still connected, that you have not reached your daily limit, and that the mode is set the way you expect. In approval mode, replies wait until you approve them.

A reply was wrong or incomplete

Add the correct answer to your Knowledge Base. Every specialist and every channel will use it from then on.

A customer says they paid but the order is unpaid

Check your payment provider's dashboard. Glime marks orders paid only after the provider confirms. Do not rely on screenshots.

I want to pause automation for a while

Switch to approval mode, lower your daily limits, or turn follow-ups off for specific leads.

How do I take over a conversation?

Open the chat in the WhatsApp or Instagram specialist and reply yourself. You can also use the Human Handoff settings to decide when Glime hands over.

Can I disconnect an account?

Yes, at any time. Glime stops reading and replying on that account as soon as it is disconnected.
