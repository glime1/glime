FAQ

Questions about the Instagram agent.

What does the Instagram agent do?

It answers every question your customers ask in your Instagram DMs, using the business details you add. Its job is to answer, build trust and bring the customer to WhatsApp.

Can the Instagram agent take payment?

No. Only the WhatsApp Sales Specialist generates and takes payment. The Instagram agent only brings the customer to WhatsApp.

When does the WhatsApp button appear?

After the customer has asked three or four questions, the agent shows a button to continue on WhatsApp.

Does WhatsApp know the customer came from Instagram?

Yes. When the customer taps the button, the WhatsApp side knows the customer came from Instagram, so the chat continues with the right context.

Are the Instagram and WhatsApp chats kept separate?

The chats stay separate, but they are linked to the same lead. You see one person with both conversations instead of two unrelated contacts.

Is there a reply time limit on Instagram?

Yes. Instagram has a 24-hour reply window after the customer's last message. If the window is closed, Instagram may not deliver new replies until the customer messages again.

Can I reply myself?

Yes. You can switch the AI mode in any chat, for example to Manual, and reply yourself at any time.
