FAQ

Questions about Anaya.

Who is Anaya?

Anaya is the GLIME AI Assistant. She talks to you like a team member and answers questions about your customers, leads, products, orders and business performance, using your own GLIME data.

Can Anaya send WhatsApp messages?

Yes, but only after you press Approve. She prepares the exact message and the list of people, shows them to you, and nothing is sent until you approve.

Can she message many customers at once?

Yes. She can send a bulk WhatsApp message after approval. How many people one approval can reach is a limit you set in Settings.

Does she tell me what to do next?

Yes. Ask her what to focus on, and she looks at your data and suggests the next steps.

Where does she get customer details?

She extracts customer details from your chats and leads herself, and decides what should be sent to whom, so you do not have to sort lists by hand.

What if a send fails?

After the send, she shows a delivery report with how many messages were sent and how many failed.

Can I export or clear my conversation with her?

Yes. The chat menu lets you start a new chat, rename, export, copy, clear or delete the conversation.
