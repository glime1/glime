How GLIME keeps your data safe
Verified identity Every request carries a signed token that GLIME checks on its own servers before anything is shown.
Your data only Access is tied to your account on the server. The app cannot pick or switch to another business.
Permissions you control What the app may read or change is set and enforced by GLIME on every single action.
Audit trail Actions taken through this connection are recorded so they can be reviewed.

Encrypted & revocable Traffic is encrypted over HTTPS, and you can disconnect the app at any time.
