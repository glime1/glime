STEP 2 · DRAFT AND APPROVE

You approve. Then it sends.

FAQ

Questions about the Follow-up agent.

What does the Follow-up agent do?

It reads your Instagram and WhatsApp chats, works out where each lead stands, and writes a follow-up draft for you.

Does it send messages by itself?

No. It sends a follow-up only after you approve it. You can read the draft, edit it, delete it, or tap Approve & Send.

How does it know what to write?

It reads the real conversation with that customer, so the draft refers to what the customer actually asked, such as the product or the price.

Which leads does it pick?

Leads whose follow-up is due or overdue, leads that went quiet, and leads that have been inactive for a while. The AI Suggestions card shows them.

Can I turn it off?

Yes. The AI Follow-up Engine has an on/off switch in your dashboard.

Does it take payment?

No. Only the WhatsApp Sales Specialist generates and takes payment. The Follow-up agent helps bring quiet leads back to the conversation.

Where can I see the results?

The Follow-up screen shows total leads, pending follow-ups, follow-ups sent, customers converted and revenue from follow-ups.
