ILLUSTRATIVE SYSTEMS

What a Glime solution could look like.

01

LOCAL SERVICE BUSINESS

Leads were arriving, but follow-ups were inconsistent.

ILLUSTRATIVE

THE PROBLEM

Enquiries were coming through multiple channels, making it difficult to consistently respond, qualify and follow up with potential customers.

THE BOTTLENECK

Manual lead handling created delays and made the follow-up process dependent on individual team members.

SYSTEM DIRECTION

Lead Capture → AI Qualification → Follow-up

POTENTIAL IMPROVEMENT

A more consistent lead-handling workflow with automated qualification and follow-up support.

02

APPOINTMENT-BASED BUSINESS

Too much time was spent managing appointments manually.

ILLUSTRATIVE

THE PROBLEM

Customers needed to ask questions, check availability and schedule appointments while staff also had to manage reminders and changes.

THE BOTTLENECK

Repetitive communication and manual scheduling created unnecessary operational work.

SYSTEM DIRECTION

Customer → AI Assistant → Booking

POTENTIAL IMPROVEMENT

Reduce repetitive appointment communication and create a more structured booking workflow.

03

GROWING BUSINESS

Business information was spread across disconnected tools.

ILLUSTRATIVE

THE PROBLEM

Customer information, conversations, tasks and follow-ups were being handled in different places.

THE BOTTLENECK

The team had to manually move information between systems, creating fragmented workflows.

SYSTEM DIRECTION

CRM ↔ AI ↔ Automation

POTENTIAL IMPROVEMENT

Create a connected operational workflow so information can move between business processes more efficiently.
