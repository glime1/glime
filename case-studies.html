ILLUSTRATIVE SCENARIOS

What a Glime setup could look like.

01

PRODUCT SELLER ON INSTAGRAM

Comments and DMs arrived faster than anyone could answer.

ILLUSTRATIVE

THE PROBLEM

A small brand posts reels and gets many "price?" comments and DMs. The owner replies late, and many interested people never hear back.

THE BOTTLENECK

Every reply, address, payment and order is handled by one person, usually in the evening after other work is done.

SYSTEM DIRECTION

Instagram → WhatsApp → Payment → Order

POTENTIAL IMPROVEMENT

Faster first replies, fewer lost enquiries, and a payment and order flow that no longer depends on one person being online.

02

SERVICE BUSINESS ON WHATSAPP

Customers asked the same questions again and again.

ILLUSTRATIVE

THE PROBLEM

Customers keep asking about prices, timings and availability, and the team repeats the same answers all day.

THE BOTTLENECK

Repetitive messages take time away from actual work, and enquiries that arrive after hours wait until the next day.

SYSTEM DIRECTION

Knowledge Base → AI Replies → Owner Approval

POTENTIAL IMPROVEMENT

Common questions answered instantly from the business's own information, with the owner approving replies until they trust the setup.

03

BUSINESS WITH REPEAT CUSTOMERS

Customers bought once and were never contacted again.

ILLUSTRATIVE

THE PROBLEM

After delivery, there is no check-in and no reminder, so past customers drift away even if they were happy.

THE BOTTLENECK

Remembering who bought what and when to follow up is manual, so it is rarely done.

SYSTEM DIRECTION

Order → Follow-up → Repeat Order

POTENTIAL IMPROVEMENT

Timely check-ins after a few days, a month and two months, giving past customers a simple way to buy again.
