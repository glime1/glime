PACKAGE FAQ

Important things to know.

Do I need to pay before starting?

No. Eligible new accounts receive the current 7-day free trial through the GLIME signup and provisioning flow.

Are the prices hard-coded on this page?

No. The billing system's active catalog is the source of truth for subscription pricing and billing.

Can I upgrade later?

Yes. GLIME's billing flow supports changing an active subscription according to the available plans and billing rules.

Is Business Custom a fixed-price plan?

No. Custom business work is scoped according to the requirements, integrations and implementation involved.

Are third-party charges included?

Not automatically. External providers, API usage, messaging charges or other third-party costs may be separate where applicable.

Where do I create my account?

Use Create Account or any package trial button. It takes you to the GLIME signup flow, not the Contact form.
