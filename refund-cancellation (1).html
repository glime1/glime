Refund & Cancellation Policy

This policy explains how the free trial, subscription cancellation, auto-renewal, upgrades, wallet top-ups, add-ons, refunds, payment issues, service suspension and custom services are handled by GLIME.

Effective date: 14 November 2026

1. Free Trial

Eligible new accounts receive a 7-day free trial through the GLIME signup flow. No card is required to start the trial, and nothing is charged unless you choose a paid plan and complete the payment yourself.

Trial allowances are provided for evaluation only. They have no cash value and are not refundable or transferable.

2. Subscription Plans, Billing & Auto-Renewal

GLIME offers paid subscription plans on a monthly or yearly billing period, as shown on the Packages page and in your Billing page at the time of purchase. The prices and billing details shown in the billing system at checkout apply to your purchase. Payments are processed through Razorpay or another payment provider used by GLIME.

Paid plans renew automatically at the end of each billing period while auto-renew is on. You can turn auto-renew off, and resume it, from your Billing page at any time.

Taxes, where applicable, are shown or applied according to the billing information presented at checkout. Invoices and payment history are available in your Billing page.

3. Subscription Cancellation

You can cancel your GLIME subscription by turning off auto-renew from your Billing page, or by contacting GLIME support. Cancellation stops future renewals.

After you cancel, your plan stays active until the end of the billing period you have already paid for (one month for a monthly plan, one year for a yearly plan). After that, paid features and plan limits no longer apply.

Cancelling does not create a refund for the current billing period or for services already made available or used, unless a refund is approved under this policy. To avoid being charged for the next period, please cancel before your renewal date.

4. Upgrades & Downgrades

You can upgrade to a higher plan from your Billing page at any time. The upgrade takes effect immediately, and the unused value of your current plan is added to your GLIME wallet as credit, as described on the Packages page.

Wallet credit given on an upgrade can be used inside GLIME. It is not paid out in cash and is not refundable, unless a refund is required by law or approved by GLIME.

If you move to a lower plan, the change applies from your next billing period. Plan limits for credits, storage and business data follow the plan that is active at that time. Moving to a lower plan does not create a refund for the current period.

5. Refund Eligibility

Because GLIME offers a free trial before you pay, charges for a subscription that has already been activated are generally non-refundable. GLIME does not promise an automatic or pro-rata refund for unused days, unused credits or unused storage.

A refund may be considered where appropriate for circumstances such as a duplicate charge, an incorrect charge, a payment captured in error, a charge made after a valid cancellation was completed, or another payment issue that GLIME confirms after review.

Where a refund is requested for another reason, GLIME may review the request based on the circumstances, the service used, the applicable order or subscription terms, and any requirements of the payment provider or applicable law. Approval of one refund does not create a general entitlement to future refunds.

6. Non-Refundable Charges

Unless otherwise required by applicable law or expressly approved by GLIME, the following are generally non-refundable:

  • a subscription period that has already started, whether monthly or yearly;
  • AI credits, storage, business data and other plan allowances already provided;
  • add-ons that have been activated;
  • wallet balance already used for WhatsApp or Instagram replies, follow-ups and broadcast messages;
  • services already delivered, usage already consumed, or work already performed;
  • unused trial allowances and rewards or grants added by GLIME to your account.

Third-party charges, transaction fees, taxes, messaging charges, or other amounts imposed by a payment provider or third-party platform may be treated separately where GLIME is not able to reverse or recover those amounts.

7. Wallet & Add-ons

Your GLIME wallet is a pay-as-you-use balance for WhatsApp and Instagram replies, follow-ups and broadcast messages. You add money to the wallet yourself, and the applicable amount is deducted as messages are used, at the rates shown on the Packages page and in your account.

Wallet top-ups are non-refundable once the payment is successful, and money already deducted for messages cannot be returned. If a top-up was charged twice, or charged but not credited to your wallet, contact us as described in section 8 and we will review it.

Add-ons (such as extra business data or storage) are billed monthly and added to your subscription. You can stop an add-on from your Billing page. Stopping an add-on prevents future charges and does not refund the period already billed.

8. Failed / Duplicate Payments

If a payment fails, including an auto-renewal payment, the service may remain unpaid or may be temporarily restricted until successful payment is received. GLIME may retry a failed renewal payment where the payment method allows it.

If the same transaction is successfully charged more than once because of a duplicate payment, or if money is deducted but the plan, add-on or wallet credit is not activated, GLIME may verify the transaction and arrange an applicable refund, credit or correction.

Please contact connect@glime.online with the registered email or mobile number, the payment date and amount, and the transaction or Razorpay payment ID.

9. Service Suspension & Termination

GLIME may suspend or terminate access where payment obligations remain unresolved, where an account is used in violation of the Terms of Service, where security or platform integrity requires action, or where continued service would create a legal or operational risk.

Suspension or termination resulting from customer misuse, prohibited activity, fraud, abuse, or policy violations does not automatically create a right to a refund.

Where appropriate, GLIME may provide notice or an opportunity to resolve an issue before suspension, but this may not be possible where immediate action is reasonably necessary.

GLIME aims to keep its service available, but does not guarantee uninterrupted operation. A temporary interruption does not by itself create a refund entitlement. GLIME may review a request in case of a prolonged outage of the core service that GLIME confirms.

10. Your Data After Cancellation or Non-Payment

Cancelling a subscription does not by itself delete your data straight away. Before your paid period ends, we recommend that you download anything you need from the Download Data page in your dashboard.

If you ask for deletion: you can request deletion at any time as explained in our Data Deletion Instructions. Requested data is deleted 30 days after the request is confirmed or verified, unless you cancel the request before then.

If you stop paying: if no successful payment is made to GLIME for 12 months after your last paid plan ended or your last successful payment, GLIME may permanently delete the data stored in that account, including conversations, leads, customers, files and business information. Where possible, GLIME will send a reminder to your registered email before this happens, but you remain responsible for downloading the data you need. Deleted data cannot be recovered.

Some billing, tax, security and payment-proof records may be kept for a limited period as described in the Data Deletion Instructions and the Privacy Policy.

11. WhatsApp / Instagram / Meta-Related Restrictions

GLIME operates as a technology provider (Meta Tech Provider) for the WhatsApp Business Platform, Instagram and related Meta services. This means GLIME provides the software that connects your business accounts to these services. The WhatsApp Business and Instagram accounts, phone numbers, templates and messaging limits belong to you and are controlled by Meta, not by GLIME.

Meta alone decides whether an account, number, template or feature is approved, limited, restricted, suspended or blocked, and Meta can change its policies, quality rules and pricing at any time. GLIME cannot override or reverse these decisions, so GLIME is not responsible for an account being restricted, blocked or banned by Meta, and such an action does not by itself create a refund entitlement.

We want your account to stay healthy, so we ask you to follow these practices:

  • message only customers who have given consent, and respect opt-out requests;
  • do not send spam, misleading, or unwanted bulk messages;
  • use approved message templates where required, and honest content;
  • keep your business details accurate and follow Meta and WhatsApp policies;
  • review your AI replies, follow-ups and broadcasts so that they follow these rules.

If Meta takes action on your account, GLIME will make reasonable efforts to help you understand the reason shown by Meta and the steps to follow, and, where Meta's process allows, to support your appeal or review. The final decision stays with Meta. GLIME does not guarantee approval, uninterrupted availability, message delivery, template approval, account standing, or continued access to third-party services.

Charges made by Meta for messages are third-party charges and follow section 6.

12. GLIME Payments

If you use GLIME Payments to collect payments from your own customers, the GLIME Payments Terms & Conditions apply to those payments, including fees, holds, payouts, refunds and chargebacks. If these terms conflict with this policy on the subject of payments you collect from your customers, the Payments Terms apply.

This policy covers what you pay to GLIME. Refunds to your own customers for their purchases from your business are handled according to the Payments Terms and your own business policies.

13. Enterprise, Custom Software & Other Services

GLIME is primarily a SaaS platform. Enterprise plans and separate services, such as custom software, custom AI agents, workflows, dashboards, integrations, website development, implementation, configuration or other professional services, may be offered and are quoted according to your requirements.

Such services may have their own scope, milestones, deliverables, payment terms and cancellation and refund conditions agreed with the customer. If a written quotation, order, statement of work or service agreement applies, those agreed terms govern the relevant service. Where that document does not address cancellation or refunds, GLIME will review the request in good faith based on the work already performed, costs already incurred and the terms agreed.

Standard SaaS subscription cancellation and refunds are governed by this policy and are not automatically governed by the commercial terms of a separate custom engagement.

14. Refund Processing

Refund requests are reviewed after GLIME verifies the relevant payment, subscription, account and service information.

When a refund is approved, GLIME will generally process it through the original payment method or payment provider used for the transaction, subject to that provider's processing requirements. GLIME may instead provide wallet credit where you agree to it.

After a refund is approved and submitted, it usually reaches the customer's account within 5 to 7 business days. The exact time depends on the payment provider, bank or card issuer, and GLIME does not control the final settlement time.

15. Chargebacks & Payment Disputes

If you have a concern about a charge, please contact GLIME first so that we can resolve it quickly. Raising a chargeback or dispute with your bank or card issuer for a charge that is valid under this policy may lead to suspension of the account while the matter is reviewed, and GLIME may respond to the dispute with the account, usage and payment records available.

16. Policy Changes

GLIME may update this Refund & Cancellation Policy when its services, plans, billing processes, payment providers, legal requirements or business practices change.

The updated version will apply from the time it is published on the GLIME website, unless a different effective date is expressly stated. A change in policy does not affect a refund request that was already approved.
