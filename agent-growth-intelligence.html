What this agent does

It sees the whole business, so you do not have to dig.

Observes everything

Customers, orders, payments, products and channels. It keeps a daily eye on all of it.

Explains what changed

A short weekly briefing says what went up, what to watch out for and why it matters.

Tells you what to do next

Clear next actions with a confidence score, based only on your own GLIME data.

Good to know: this is a separate system from the chat agents. It does not talk to your customers. It watches your data and advises you. You can ask the AI Assistant about any card with the Ask Anaya button.

FAQ

Questions about Growth Intelligence.

What does the Growth Intelligence agent do?

It watches your leads, orders and payments and turns them into clear answers: how the week went, what is selling, who may leave, when customers are active, and what to do next.

Is it connected to the chat agents?

It is a separate system. It does not chat with customers. It observes the data that the other agents and your dashboard create, and it advises you.

Can it tell me the best time to message customers?

Yes. The Best Time card shows when customers write, convert and order, so you can send follow-ups and offers just before those times.

Does it look at competitors and the market?

Yes. You can add the competitors you know or let AI find them, and refresh Market Demand to see what customers in your area and category are asking for.

Does it make up numbers?

No. Every number comes from your own GLIME data. Where data is missing, it says so instead of guessing.

Can I get the weekly briefing in Hindi?

Yes. The Weekly AI Briefing can be switched between English and Hindi.

What is Ask Anaya on each card?

Each card has an Ask Anaya button. It lets you ask the AI Assistant a question about that exact card, such as why revenue went down.
