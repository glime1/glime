Security & compliance

Security that follows Indian law.

Customer conversations are personal data. GLIME is built to protect them and to follow the data protection rules that apply in India.

Protection built in

  • Data is held on secure cloud infrastructure
  • Secure authentication, access controls and data protection
  • No one browses your conversations. The GLIME team reviews only security alerts, and only the minimum needed, to keep the platform and every customer safe.

Misuse is stopped automatically

  • Automatic detection flags spam and misuse
  • Accounts that misuse the platform are blocked automatically
  • After a block, a review window opens so the owner can submit the case for review

Indian rules we build to

  • Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023, and the DPDP Rules, 2025, which roll out in phases
  • Information Technology Act, 2000, and the SPDI Rules, 2011
  • CERT-In cyber security directions, 2022

FAQ

Questions businesses usually ask.

What do I get in the 7-day free trial?

You get 7 days to try GLIME with 10,000 AI credits, 100 Instagram messages and 50 WhatsApp messages. Eligible trial modules are provisioned after signup verification.

Do I need a card to start?

No. You can start the free trial without a card.

Which channels and tools can I connect?

GLIME works with Instagram and WhatsApp Business, along with Shopify and Razorpay. Availability may vary by plan and setup.

Which languages does GLIME reply in?

GLIME understands Hindi, English and Hinglish and replies in the language your customer uses.

Do I need technical knowledge?

No. You can explain your business in normal language, and GLIME helps set up the right workflow around it.

Is my data secure?

GLIME is built with secure authentication, access controls and data protection on cloud infrastructure, and is built to follow India's data protection rules, including the DPDP Act 2023, the IT Act 2000 and the SPDI Rules 2011. See the Security section above for details.

How much does GLIME cost after the trial?

GLIME Growth is ₹299 a month or ₹2,999 a year. GLIME Omni is ₹1,499 a month or ₹15,999 a year. Your wallet is charged only for the replies your AI sends: ₹0.02 per WhatsApp message and ₹0.01 per Instagram message. Third-party charges, such as WhatsApp messaging fees, may be separate.

How long does setup take?

You can set up GLIME in about 10 to 15 minutes. Create your account, connect WhatsApp or Instagram, add your business details and turn on your AI agents.

Can I start earning from the very first message?

Yes. From your first customer message, GLIME can run the whole flow automatically: reply, share a payment link, record the order and follow up. Payments go through Razorpay, and the money goes directly to your own account once you connect it.

Can I control how much AI GLIME uses?

Yes. In Settings, under Automation, you choose how many AI tokens each channel may use per day and per month. Tokens are what the AI spends to read and write replies. You can turn a limit off if you do not want one.

Will GLIME send messages without my approval?

Your sales agents reply to customers on their own, using only your business details. The AI Assistant drafts messages and sends them only after you approve, and follow-up drafts can wait for your review.

What happens if the AI does not know the answer?

GLIME answers only from the details you give it, so it does not guess. Handoff rules in your AI settings cover the rest: for example when a customer asks for a person, or the AI has no approved answer. The customer gets a short message and your team follows up.

Does GLIME use Meta's official platform?

Yes. GLIME is a Meta Tech Provider. WhatsApp Business and Instagram connect through Meta's own platform, with access you approve.

Can I use GLIME on my phone?

Yes. GLIME works in your phone's browser, and you can add it to your phone as an app using Install App in the dashboard menu.

Can I cancel, and how do refunds work?

You can cancel from your billing controls or by contacting support. Cancellation stops future renewals. A refund is not automatic for a period that has already started, but duplicate or incorrect charges are reviewed. See the Refund and Cancellation page for the full policy.

How do I delete my data?

Open Download Data in your dashboard and choose Delete my business data. Deletion is scheduled for 30 days later, and you can cancel before then. You can also email connect@glime.online. See the Data Deletion page for details.

Can I earn by recommending GLIME?

Yes. The Partner program is free to join and pays up to 30% recurring commission on subscription fees GLIME actually receives from customers you refer. Earnings are not guaranteed. Agencies can also manage many clients from one dashboard at wholesale prices.
