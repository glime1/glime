Frequently Asked Questions

Do you use WordPress or No-Code tools?
Never! No-code tools like WordPress are slow, easily hackable, and heavily restricted. Every website at Glime is 100% hand-coded from scratch using VS Code, ensuring ultra-fast speed, absolute security, and zero limitations.
Is the Domain & Hosting cost included?
Yes! Our packages include free hosting setup. For domain names, we guide you on how to purchase your own domain (usually ₹500-₹800/year) so you have 100% ownership of your brand name.
How many days will it take to build my growth engine?
For our 'Launch Edition' package, we usually deliver within 3-5 working days. The 'Growth Pro Engine' package takes about 7-10 days depending on your requirements.
What if I need changes after the website is live?
Don't worry! We offer a dedicated 'Maintenance & Growth' plan at just ₹500/month. This covers regular text updates, changing images, and ensuring your system is always secure.
