FAQ

Questions businesses usually ask.

What kind of business problems can Glime solve?

Glime focuses on problems involving repetitive work, lead handling, customer communication, follow-ups, appointments and business workflows.

Do I need technical knowledge?

No. You can explain the business problem in normal language. Glime can help translate that problem into a possible technology approach.

Can Glime work with my existing software?

Where technically suitable, existing tools and software can be incorporated into the proposed workflow. The exact integrations depend on the project.

Can you build a custom AI system?

Yes. Custom systems can be designed around the specific business problem, workflow and requirements.

How does implementation work?

We start by understanding the problem, diagnose the bottleneck, design the solution and then implement the required workflows and systems.

Do you provide ongoing support?

Ongoing maintenance and optimization can be provided depending on the selected service and project scope.
