PACKAGE FAQ

Important things to know.

Do I need to pay before starting?

No. Eligible new accounts receive the current 7-day free trial through the GLIME signup and provisioning flow.

Are the prices on this page final?

The Billing page is the source of truth. The prices here follow it, and if anything ever differs, the price shown on your Billing page is the one that applies.

Can I upgrade later?

Yes. You can upgrade from the Billing page at any time. The upgrade is immediate and the unused value of your current plan is kept as wallet credit.

How does pay-as-you-use work?

You buy AI credits in packs from your Billing page and use them as your AI replies to customers. Messages inside the 24-hour window are not charged, so you only top up credits when you need more AI.

What are AI credits?

1 credit equals 1 AI token. Every AI reply uses credits based on the length of the conversation and the reply. When credits run out, AI auto-replies pause until you buy more, and you can still reply manually.

What is the 24-hour window?

Within 24 hours of a customer's last message, you can send unlimited WhatsApp and Instagram messages with no per-message charge, and your AI replies using only AI credits. On Growth, AI replies are possible only inside this window. On Omni, you can also message customers after 24 hours and send broadcasts, billed from your wallet as marketing messages at ₹1.20 or utility messages at ₹0.80 each.

What is the Appointment Agent?

It books appointments from your calendar slots, confirms them with the customer and sends reminders. It is one of the six Business Specialists included in Growth and Omni.

What is the AI-powered website?

GLIME builds a website for your business with AI. The services and products you add in GLIME show up on it automatically, with their photos and prices. When a customer taps Buy, WhatsApp opens with the product details already filled in, so the conversation reaches your GLIME WhatsApp agent right away. Your site goes live on a GLIME web address.

Do my customers get an invoice automatically?

Yes. Once a customer's payment is received, GLIME sends an order confirmation on WhatsApp followed by a PDF invoice. It carries your logo, business details, the items, the amount paid and the order number. It is sent while the customer's 24-hour WhatsApp window is open.

What is the difference between marketing and utility messages?

Marketing messages are promotional, such as offers and win-back follow-ups. Utility messages are about something the customer already did, such as an order, booking or payment update.

What does Unlimited / Custom mean on Enterprise?

Enterprise limits are agreed around your business. Credits, storage and business data can be unlimited or set to the level you need, and the details are confirmed in your custom plan.

Is Enterprise a fixed-price plan?

No. Enterprise is scoped according to your requirements, integrations and implementation, and quoted separately.

Can I cancel auto-renew?

Yes. You can turn auto-renew off and resume it again from your Billing page whenever you like.

Are third-party charges included?

Not automatically. External providers, API usage, messaging charges or other third-party costs may be separate where applicable.

Where do I create my account?

Use Create Account or any package trial button. It takes you to the GLIME signup flow, not the Contact form.
