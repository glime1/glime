HOW YOUR TEAM WORKS

From the first message to money in your account.

When a customer messages your business, your team takes it from there and passes the work along, step by step. This is the path every conversation follows.

  1. Receive

    The message arrives on WhatsApp or Instagram through Meta's official platform.

  2. Understand

    GLIME works out what the customer wants: a price, a size, a booking, an order.

  3. Answer

    The reply is built from your own products, prices and services, then sent in seconds.

  4. Close

    Payment details are shared, the payment is tracked and the order is recorded with the customer's details.

  5. Follow up and learn

    Follow-ups go out on time, and your growth insight updates with what actually converted.
