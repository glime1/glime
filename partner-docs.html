Overview

The GLIME Partner Program lets you earn recurring commission by recommending GLIME to businesses that sell and support customers on WhatsApp and Instagram. You share a personal referral link. When a business signs up through it and starts a paid plan, you earn a percentage of what GLIME receives from that customer for life, for as long as they stay subscribed.

Partner accounts are separate from client accounts. You sign in to the partner dashboard with Google. It does not give you access to any GLIME client workspace, and a client login does not give access to the partner dashboard.

Getting started

  1. Join with Google

    Open the Partner Program page and choose Continue with Google. Your partner account is created immediately and the partner dashboard opens.

  2. Find your referral link

    In the dashboard, open Referral link. Copy your personal link, or add an optional campaign tag to see which channel works best.

  3. Share it

    Send it to clients, colleagues or your audience. The dashboard includes ready-to-use messages you can adjust to your own voice.

  4. Add payout details

    Open Payouts and save a UPI ID or bank account so your commission can be paid as soon as it is available.

  5. Track results

    Follow clicks, sign-ups, active customers and earnings in Overview, Referrals and Earnings.

Tip. Recommend GLIME to people who already receive many enquiries on WhatsApp or Instagram. They get the most value, and they are more likely to stay subscribed.

Commission & tiers

How commission is calculated

You earn a percentage of the subscription fees GLIME actually receives from each customer attributed to you, for life: every month that customer stays on a paid plan, starting from their first paid payment. Commission is calculated on the amount received after taxes (such as GST), refunds, chargebacks and third-party payment fees.

Lifetime commission

  • Commission continues every month for as long as the referred customer remains on a paid plan and attributed to you. There is no 12 or 24 month limit.
  • It stops when the customer cancels or their payments stop. Commission on a refunded or reversed payment is reversed too.
  • "Lifetime" means the life of that customer's subscription, not a guarantee of any amount. Program terms can change, and changes apply going forward.

Referral tracking

How a referral is attributed

  • When someone opens your referral link, GLIME stores your partner code in their browser for 60 days.
  • If they create a GLIME account within that window, the new account is attributed to you.
  • If someone clicks more than one partner's link, the most recent click within the window is credited.
  • Only new GLIME accounts qualify. Existing customers cannot be attributed to a partner.

Things that can affect tracking

Private browsing, cleared cookies, blockers or switching device between the click and the sign-up can prevent attribution. If you believe a referral was missed, contact us with the customer's business name and the date they signed up, and we will review it.

Campaign tags

Add a short tag such as instagram-bio to your link to see how each place you share performs. Tags do not change your commission.

  • Taxes: commission may be subject to applicable taxes. You are responsible for your own tax obligations. GLIME may ask for tax details where the law requires it.

Brand guidelines

You are welcome to talk about GLIME. Please do it in a way that is accurate and recognisable as your own recommendation.

Do

  • Write GLIME in capital letters.
  • Describe GLIME as a tool that helps businesses manage WhatsApp and Instagram conversations, leads, orders and follow-ups.
  • Mention current plan prices only as listed on the GLIME website, and say prices can change.
  • Be clear that you are a GLIME partner and earn commission.

Don't

  • Promise income, sales or results that GLIME does not guarantee.
  • Present yourself as GLIME, an employee of GLIME or an official GLIME representative.
  • Alter, distort or recolour the GLIME logo.
  • Use GLIME, its logo or a confusingly similar name in your own business name, domain or social handle.

Program policies

These rules keep the program fair for customers, for GLIME and for every partner.

Not allowed

  • Self-referral. Signing up yourself, or through an account you own or control, using your own link.
  • Spam. Unsolicited bulk messages, mass tagging, or posting your link in places where it is not welcome.
  • Misleading claims. False statements about GLIME, its pricing, features, results or about your relationship with GLIME.
  • Paid search on GLIME terms. Bidding on "GLIME" or close variations in paid search advertising.
  • Incentive abuse. Offering cash or gifts to people to sign up, creating fake accounts, or any attempt to inflate clicks, sign-ups or payments.
  • Unlawful content. Sharing your link alongside unlawful, hateful or adult content.

What happens if a rule is broken

GLIME may withhold or reverse commission connected to the violation, suspend your partner account, or remove you from the program. Where we can, we will tell you why.

Frequently asked questions

Does it cost anything to join?

No. Joining is free and there are no sales targets.

Do I need to be a GLIME customer?

No. Partner accounts are separate from client accounts.

How long do I earn commission on a customer?

For life. You keep earning every month for as long as the customer stays on a paid plan and attributed to you. It stops only when they cancel or stop paying.

Which sign-in methods are supported?

Google. Use the same Google account each time to reach the same partner dashboard.

When does a customer count towards my tier?

When they are actively paying and attributed to you. Customers on a free trial, or who have cancelled, are not counted.

Why is my commission pending?

New commission is held for 30 days to cover the refund window. It then becomes available and is paid on the next payout date.

What if my balance is below the minimum?

It rolls over. It will be paid once your available balance reaches ₹1,000.

Can I run ads for my link?

Yes, within the policies. Do not bid on GLIME brand terms in paid search, and keep all claims accurate.

A customer I referred is not showing up. What should I do?

Check that they signed up within 60 days of clicking your link on the same browser. If it still looks wrong, contact us with their business name and sign-up date.

How do I stop being a partner?

Stop sharing your link and contact us to close your partner account. Earned, non-reversed commission is still paid.

Glossary

Partner code
The short unique code in your referral link that identifies you.
Referral
A business that signed up for GLIME through your link.
Active customer
A referral that is currently on a paid GLIME plan.
Attribution
Crediting a sign-up to the partner whose link led to it.
Lifetime commission
Commission that continues every month for as long as a referred customer stays on a paid plan.
Pending
Commission that is still in the hold period.
Available
Commission that cleared the hold period and is ready to be paid.
Rollover
An available balance below the minimum payout, carried to the next month.
Tier
Your commission level, based on active paying customers.
