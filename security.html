01

Our approach

GLIME is built so that each business sees only its own data, access requires a signed-in account, and sensitive actions happen on the server rather than in the browser. We keep the design simple and review it as the product grows.

SEC
Honest about limits

No online service can promise perfect security. We describe what we do, not guarantees we cannot make, and we improve it over time.

02

How data is protected

Separate data for every business Database access rules make sure a signed-in business can read and change only its own records, such as services, customers, leads and orders.
Sign-in required Accounts use secure authentication. Server-side functions check your signed-in session before they act, and your business is identified from your account, not from anything typed in the browser.
Two-factor authentication Add a second step to sign-in. Once 2FA is on, every login asks for a 6-digit code from an authenticator app on your phone, as well as your password.
Tokens stay on the server Access tokens and keys for your connected channels are kept on GLIME’s servers. They are not stored in your browser and are never shown on the page.
Encrypted connections GLIME is served over HTTPS, so data moving between your device and our servers is encrypted in transit.
Private file storage Images, videos and documents you upload are kept in private storage and shown through temporary links rather than public addresses.
Managed cloud infrastructure GLIME runs on Supabase for database, authentication and storage, hosted in the Singapore region. Supabase applies its own platform security controls, including encryption of stored data.
Usage limits you control Set daily limits for WhatsApp and Instagram automation, so activity on your account stays within bounds you choose.
Restricted admin access Administrative areas are limited to authorized GLIME staff accounts.
03

WhatsApp & Instagram connections

Used for your features

Channel data is used to show conversations, send replies and run the sales features you turned on. See the Privacy Policy.

04

AI and your data

GLIME AI answers customers using the services you publish and the knowledge you add for your business. Your business knowledge is kept under your account and is not used to answer customers of other businesses. To write a reply, GLIME sends the text it needs to the AI technology providers described in the next sections, so you can see exactly where data goes.

01

You control sending

Choose Auto, AI + Approval or Manual mode so you decide how much the AI sends on its own.

02

You control the limits

Set daily limits for WhatsApp and Instagram in Settings → Automation.

03

You control the content

Edit or delete services and knowledge at any time. Removing a service deletes it with its pricing, variants and media.

04

You stay in the loop

AI can occasionally make mistakes. For important or sensitive replies, use AI + Approval or Manual mode so a person checks the message before it is sent.

06

What AI receives, and what it never does

Only what is needed to produce a reply is sent to an AI provider. We do not send your whole account, and some things are never sent at all.

Sent to AI providers, only as needed

  • The customer’s message text
  • Recent conversation context, so the reply makes sense
  • The services, prices and knowledge you added to GLIME that relate to the question
  • The instructions and tone settings you chose for your AI

Never sent to AI providers

  • Passwords or login credentials
  • Payment card or bank details
  • API keys and access tokens, including your channel connections
  • Data that belongs to other businesses
07

Life of a message

Here is what happens, step by step, when a customer writes to your business and AI helps reply.

CUSTOMER → META → GLIME SERVER → AI PROVIDER → GLIME SERVER → CUSTOMER
01

The customer sends a message

They write to your WhatsApp or Instagram account, exactly as they would to any business.

02

Meta delivers it to GLIME

The message reaches GLIME over an encrypted connection, through the channel you connected.

03

GLIME identifies your business

Our server works out which business the message belongs to, loads only that business’s services and knowledge, and applies your mode and daily limits.

04

The AI provider drafts a reply

The text needed for the answer is sent to an AI provider over an encrypted connection. The provider returns a draft reply to GLIME.

05

The reply is sent or held for you

In Auto mode the reply goes out automatically. In AI + Approval or Manual mode it waits for you. The conversation is stored under your account only.

08

Training, retention and location

These are the questions clients ask us most about AI and data.

No training on your data GLIME uses the commercial business services of its AI providers. Under those commercial terms, content sent through these services is not used to train the providers’ models.
Provider handling Providers may process requests, and may keep them for a limited time for safety and abuse monitoring, as described in their own terms. Their policies apply to that handling.
Where data is processed GLIME’s database, sign-in and file storage run in the Singapore region on Supabase. AI providers process requests on their own global infrastructure, which may be outside India, so AI requests can cross borders over encrypted connections.

For the legal details of how we collect and use personal data, see our Privacy Policy.

10

Your responsibilities

Security is shared. GLIME protects the platform, and these habits protect your account the most:

01

Make your password strong

Build a long, hard-to-guess password that you do not use anywhere else. Never share it, or your account, with anyone.

02

Turn on two-factor authentication

Go to Settings → Security and enable 2FA. Every login will then need a 6-digit code from your authenticator app. Keep your phone safe: if you lose access to the app, contact connect@glime.online to reset 2FA.

03

Protect your devices

Keep your phone and computer locked and updated, and log out on shared devices.

04

Control who has access

Do not hand your login to others, and change your password when someone leaves your team.

05

Watch for phishing

GLIME will never ask for your password or an OTP by message. Check the address says glime.online before you sign in.

06

Spot unusual activity early

If you see messages you did not send or sign-ins you do not recognize, change your password straight away and contact us.

07

Respect your customers’ data

You decide what your customers are asked to share in chats. Avoid collecting sensitive details such as OTPs or card numbers in a conversation, let your customers know that AI helps answer messages, and follow the laws that apply to your business, including India’s Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023.

11

If something goes wrong

If we confirm a security incident that affects your data, we will investigate, take steps to contain it, and tell affected clients as required by applicable law. If you notice anything unusual in your account, such as messages you did not send or sign-ins you do not recognize, change your password and contact us straight away.

13

Quick answers

Q1

Is my data used to train AI models?

No. GLIME uses the commercial services of its AI providers, and under those terms content sent through them is not used to train the providers’ models.

Q2

Can another business see my data or knowledge?

No. Each business’s records and knowledge are kept separate, and your knowledge is never used to answer another business’s customers.

Q3

Does GLIME send passwords or payment details to AI?

No. Passwords, payment card or bank details, and API keys or tokens are never sent to AI providers.

Q4

Can I stop the AI from replying on its own?

Yes. Switch to AI + Approval or Manual mode, set daily limits, or disconnect a channel at any time.

Q5

Is two-factor authentication available?

Yes. Turn it on in Settings → Security. Once enabled, every login asks for a 6-digit code from your authenticator app as well as your password.

Q6

How will I know if your AI providers change?

We update this page whenever the list changes, and the “Last updated” date at the top shows when.

Report a vulnerability

If you believe you found a security weakness in GLIME, please tell us privately so we can fix it before it can be misused. Include what you found, the page or feature, and the steps to reproduce it.
