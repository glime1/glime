From application to your first introduction

Four steps. Most of the work is ours.

  1. You apply

    Tell us about your company, what you buy and which languages you work in. It takes about five minutes.

  2. We verify

    Our team checks your registration, licence and online presence. We may ask you for documents.

  3. You are listed

    Once approved, your company appears in the directory that Indian businesses on GLIME can search.

  4. Introductions arrive

    Suppliers who match your categories ask to be introduced, and the conversation happens in your language.

What we check before listing you

Indian suppliers rely on this directory, so every application is reviewed by a person at GLIME. We look at:

  • Business registration or trade licence
  • Company website or online presence
  • Contact person, email and phone number
  • Whether your business activity matches the product categories you chose

Questions distributors ask first

Can I apply from any country?

Yes. Importers, distributors, wholesalers and agents from every country can apply.

Who can see my listing?

Only Indian businesses that GLIME has approved for the Export Network. Your phone number and email are not shown in the listing.

How is my business verified?

A person on our team reviews your registration or trade licence, your website or online presence and your contact details. We may ask for documents, and we may call or email you.

What if my application is not approved?

We will tell you by email. If we could not verify something, you can send the missing details and apply again.

Will I be contacted by a machine?

Indian suppliers may use GLIME's AI assistant to write messages for them, in the language you chose. Every message comes through GLIME, and you can ask us to stop at any time.

Can I change or remove my listing later?

Yes. Write to us from the Contact page and quote your reference number.
